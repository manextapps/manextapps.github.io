One developer, a few small apps

I make the kind of apps I want on my own phone: quick to open, no account, and nothing they don't need. Questions or ideas go straight to me at manextapps@gmail.com, and I read every one.

Besides the apps, this site has puzzles and calculators that run in your browser. Two good places to start: the bulls and cows solver, which lists every code that still fits your guesses, and the 52 week savings challenge calculator, which builds a dated chart you can print. If you'd rather solve on paper, the printable logic puzzles page makes a sheet of six puzzles with the answers on a second page.
