You can type centimeters or meters too, like 1.6 cm or 0.5 m, and a comma works as the decimal point. The table under the answer shows the same size at every precision, because the nearest 1/16″ mark is sometimes a long way off and the nearest 1/32″ is right on.

How to turn millimeters into a fraction of an inch

An inch is exactly 25.4 mm. That's been the definition since 1959, so the conversion never drifts. It takes two steps:

  1. Divide by 25.4. 16 mm ÷ 25.4 = 0.62992 inches.
  2. Turn the decimal into marks. Multiply by the marks per inch you work to, round to a whole number, and put it over that number. For 16ths: 0.62992 × 16 = 10.08, which rounds to 10, so 10/16, which reduces to 5/8″.

Then check how far off it is. 5/8″ is 15.875 mm, so the mark is 0.125 mm short of 16 mm. That's less than the width of a pencil line, so 5/8 is a fine answer for 16 mm in any wood shop. For a sliding fit on a steel pin, it isn't.

Sizes over 25.4 mm work the same way. 30 mm ÷ 25.4 = 1.1811″. Keep the 1, then 0.1811 × 16 = 2.9, which rounds to 3, so 1 3/16″.

Three pairs are very close: 5/16″ and 8 mm are 0.06 mm apart, 3/4″ and 19 mm 0.05 mm, and 7/16″ and 11 mm 0.11 mm. The others are 0.13 mm or more apart, and some are close to half a millimeter: 3/8″ is 0.48 mm smaller than 10 mm, 11/16″ is 0.46 mm bigger than 17 mm, and 1″ is 0.4 mm bigger than 25 mm. Whether that's close enough depends on the job, so check the fit before you trust a swap.

Finding the mark on a tape

Most tapes in the US are marked in 16ths, so that's where the converter starts. For 5/8″, find the half inch line, then count two of the small 16th marks past it. For 15/16″, go to the next inch line and come back one small mark. If the fractions on the blade are the hard part, the tape measure trainer drills them on a drawn tape.

A lot of tapes have millimeters along the other edge. If yours does, you don't need any of this for measuring. The conversion matters when a plan, a part or a store gives you one system and your tools are in the other.

Questions

What is 16 mm in inches as a fraction?

5/8″, which is 15.875 mm, 0.125 mm short. In decimals, 16 mm is 0.62992″.

What is 18 mm as a fraction of an inch?

23/32″ to the nearest 32nd (18.256 mm). On a tape marked in 16ths the nearest mark is 11/16″, which is 0.54 mm short, so 23/32 is the better answer if you can read it.

What is 3 mm in inches as a fraction?

1/8″, which is 3.175 mm, 0.18 mm long.

What is 30 mm as a fraction of an inch?

1 3/16″, which is 30.16 mm. That's the nearest mark at 16ths, 32nds and 64ths.

Is 15.88 mm 5/8 inch?

Yes, as near as anyone can measure. 5/8″ is 15.875 mm exactly, so 15.88 is that number rounded to two places. 23.81 mm is 15/16″ the same way.

How do I go from a fraction back to millimeters?

Divide the fraction and multiply by 25.4. 3/16″ is 0.1875 × 25.4 = 4.7625 mm. The inch fraction chart lists every 64th of an inch with its millimeters.
