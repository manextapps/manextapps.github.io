How a 75 ball bingo card is built

A 75 ball card has five columns under the letters B, I, N, G and O, and each column draws only from its own 15 numbers. That's why a caller says "N 42" and not just "42". You know where to look before you hear the number.

Which numbers go in each column
ColumnNumbersOn a card
B1 to 155
I16 to 305
N31 to 454, plus the FREE square
G46 to 605
O61 to 755

The centre square is FREE and counts as marked from the start, so the N column only needs four numbers. A number never shows up twice on one card, and the numbers in a column aren't sorted. Here's card 14, exactly as it prints:

Its B column holds 14, 8, 1, 10, 5, and every one of them is between 1 and 15. Check any other column against the table and it'll fit too.

How many different bingo cards are there?

More than you'd ever print. Each of the B, I, G and O columns can be filled 360,360 ways, because you pick 5 of 15 numbers and the order top to bottom matters. The N column has 32,760 ways to fill its four spots. Multiply all five and you get 552,446,474,061,128,648,601,600,000 different 75 ball cards.

That counts two cards with the same numbers in a different order as different, which is fair, because they win different lines. If you only care which numbers are on a card, as in a full card game, it's 111,007,923,832,370,565 different sets. Either way, two players won't hold the same card by chance.

This page and the app use a fixed list of 999 cards for each game type. I checked all 999 of the 75 ball cards: no two are alike, and every number sits in its own column's range. The build of this page runs that check again every time.

Cards that match the Bingo Caller app

Every numbered card here has a number from 1 to 999, printed in its corner. The same number makes the same card in my Bingo Caller app for Android. When someone shouts bingo, open Check card in the app, type their card number, and it marks every ball called so far and tells you if the card wins.

Players can also open My card on their own phone, type the number from their paper card and tap numbers as they're called. You don't need the app to use these cards. A caller sheet and a pen work fine, and the next section has a printable one.

The caller sheet and calling slips

Print the caller sheet if you'll call the numbers by hand. Page one has every ball in a grid, laid out like a caller's board, so you can cross each one off as it's called. Under it there's a short table to write down each winner's card number and the pattern they won with.

The calling slips are the same numbers again, one per box, to cut out and draw from a bowl or a hat. For word bingo the sheet lists your words in alphabetical order with a tick box each, and the slips hold one word each.

90 ball tickets and 30 ball cards

90 ball bingo is the usual game in the UK. A ticket has 3 rows and 9 columns. Each row has 5 numbers and 4 blanks, so a ticket holds 15 numbers.

Column one takes 1 to 9, column two 10 to 19, and so on up to the last column, which takes 80 to 90. The numbers in each column go from smallest at the top to largest at the bottom. Games go one line, then two lines, then a full house.

One honest difference from a bought book of tickets: those are often printed in strips of six that use each of the 90 numbers exactly once. These tickets are numbered one at a time to match the app, so a page of six won't have that property. It doesn't change how the game plays.

30 ball is a quick game for young players or a short break: a 3 by 3 card of 9 numbers from 1 to 30, sorted, and the first full card wins.

How to run a bingo game

  1. Hand out the cards.

    One or two cards each is plenty for a first game. If you'll check winners by card number, jot down which numbers you gave out.

  2. Say the pattern before you start.

    A line in any direction is quick. Four corners and an X take longer, and a full card can run past 50 calls. Saying it out loud first saves arguments later.

  3. Call at an even pace.

    For 75 ball, say the letter and the number, like "B 7". Give people a few seconds to look. Cross each ball off the caller sheet as you go, or let the app keep the board.

  4. Check before you cheer.

    When someone calls bingo, read their numbers back and check each one against the called list. With the app, type the card number into Check card instead.

  5. Reset and go again.

    Cards with dabbers or pens are single use. Slip them into clear sleeves and use a dry wipe pen, or use buttons or coins as markers, and the same cards last all night.

Word bingo for a class, a party or a shower

Pick Your own words and type a list: spelling words, names of the people at a baby shower, sight words, things you might see on a road trip. Each card puts 24 of them around a FREE centre, and a word appears once per card at most.

Here's a catch with short lists. With exactly 24 words, every card holds the same 24, just in different spots. Lines still come at different times, but in a full card game everybody finishes on the same call. If you want full card games, give it 35 words or more so the cards differ in which words they hold too.

Short phrases work, up to 40 characters each. Long ones print smaller to fit the square, so keep them short when you can. The caller sheet's word list and slips come from the same list.

Printing tips

  • 4 cards a page suits most games and saves paper. 1 a page makes a big card for a classroom wall or for players with weaker eyesight.
  • 2 a page prints sideways, with the cards next to each other. Your printer may ask you to turn the page, but the print window should pick landscape on its own.
  • Cardstock lasts longer than plain paper if you'll reuse the cards.
  • Choose Save as PDF in the print window to keep a file you can print again or send to someone.

Questions

Can I print the same cards again later?

Yes. Numbered cards depend only on the game and the card number, so card 14 is always card 14. For a whole set, copy the link with the button under the settings. It holds the game, the card numbers, the page layout and, for word bingo, your words and set code.

Are there any repeated cards in a set?

No. The 999 numbered cards of each game are all different, and word cards are checked as they're made, so no two in a set match.

How many cards can I print at once?

Up to 100 at a time. To print more numbered cards, run it again starting from the next number, like 101 after 1 to 100. Numbers stop at 999.

Do I need the app?

No. The cards and the caller sheet work on their own. The Bingo Caller app adds a voice that calls each ball and a winner check by card number.

Can I play for money with these?

I made them for home, class and club games. Rules about bingo for money vary a lot from place to place, so check yours if you want to do that.

Why does my word card have smaller text in some squares?

Each square shrinks its text until the whole word or phrase fits. A long phrase gets small type, so shorter entries print bigger and are easier to read across a room.
