Reporting a bug

A few details make a bug much easier to fix:

  • The app's name and version. You'll find it in the app's Settings, or on its Google Play page.
  • Your phone model and Android version.
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot or a short screen recording is perfect.
  • For a puzzle game, the level number or the date of the daily puzzle.

Please don't send passwords or payment details. I'll never ask for them.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases in the apps, like Remove Ads, go through Google Play. If you get a new phone, use Restore purchase in the app's Settings.

Refunds are handled by Google Play from your order history. You're welcome to email me as well.

Privacy questions

Each app has its own privacy policy. You'll find the links at the bottom of every page and on each app's page. For this website, see the site privacy policy.
