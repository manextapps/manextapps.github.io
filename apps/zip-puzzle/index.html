Zip Puzzle: Number Path Game

Draw one line through every cell of the grid and hit the numbers in order. That's the whole rule, and every puzzle has exactly one solution.

How it plays

Start on 1 and drag. Your line has to pass the numbers in order, 1, 2, 3 and on to the last one, and it has to fill every cell along the way. Took a wrong turn? Slide back over your line, or tap Undo as often as you like.

Harder boards add short walls between some cells. That's where it gets interesting, because now you have to plan the route before you commit to it. Every board is generated on your phone and checked by a solver, so there's always exactly one path that works.

Want to try one first? There's a Zip board you can play in your browser, with a strategy guide and a worked example under it.

What's in the game

Levels that grow with you

You start on small 5x5 boards and work up to big 9x9 grids. The levels keep coming, the paths get longer, and walls show up as you get better. Your progress and best times are saved as you go.

Daily puzzle

There's one daily board, and it's the same for everyone. It starts small on Monday and grows to 9x9 by Sunday. Solve it each day to keep your streak.

Quick play

Pick a size from 5x5 to 9x9 and Easy, Medium or Hard. A fresh puzzle is ready in a moment.

Three looks

Daylight is a warm light board with a coral path. Midnight is dark with a glowing path, and it switches on with dark mode. Segment colors gives each stretch between two numbers its own color, which makes long paths a lot easier to read.

Questions

Is Zip Puzzle free?

Yes, it's free to download and supported by ads. A hint costs a short optional video, and if no video is available you still get the hint. A full screen ad may show up after some solved puzzles, never while you're solving one. One purchase removes the ads between puzzles.

Are Undo and Reset limited?

No. They're always free, as often as you want.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every mode works without a connection, and there's no account or sign in.

Which languages does it support?

Zip Puzzle is available in 15 languages.
