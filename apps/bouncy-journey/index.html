Bouncy Journey: Platformer

Run, jump and bounce across floating islands in a bright 2D platformer. Three animal heroes, ten levels, and it plays without an internet connection.

  • Android 8.0 or newer
  • Works offline
  • No account
  • Free with ads

Three heroes, three ways through

Pick Fox, Wolf or Lizard and race through levels full of springs, moving platforms, crumbling bridges, hidden coins and spike traps. Every hero can finish every level, but each one finds a different path:

  • Fox is the fastest runner, made for long, daring jumps.
  • Wolf bounces highest on springs and smashes cracked stone blocks to open shortcuts.
  • Lizard double jumps and grips walls to climb where the others cannot.

Replaying a level with a different hero feels like a new level, which is the point.

What is in the game

  • 10 levels, from a gentle tutorial to a tricky finale
  • Checkpoints, so one slip never sends you all the way back
  • Up to 3 stars per level: beat the clock and collect every coin
  • Springs, moving and falling platforms, breakable blocks and surprise traps
  • Touch controls you can resize and fade, plus keyboard and gamepad support
  • Cheerful music and sound effects, with separate switches for each

Questions

Is Bouncy Journey free?

Yes, it is free to download. Ads show after levels and on some restarts. A single Remove Ads purchase removes all of them.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can play on a plane, on the bus or anywhere without a connection. Progress is saved on your phone.

Can I use a controller?

Yes. Besides touch controls, the game supports a keyboard and a gamepad.

How long is it?

There are ten levels. Finishing them takes an evening or two, and earning three stars on each one with every hero takes a good deal longer.
